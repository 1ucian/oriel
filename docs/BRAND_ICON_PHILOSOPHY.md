# Projected Quiet

Projected Quiet treats depth as identity. A restrained geometric aperture sits forward from its origin, creating a precise sense of access, distance, and return without literal illustration. The form should read instantly at icon scale while rewarding close inspection through careful perspective and alignment.

The palette moves from midnight navy into controlled indigo, with a single cool luminous edge. Color is structural rather than decorative: darkness establishes trust and privacy, while the projected light communicates a secure connection across space. Every transition must feel deliberately calibrated.

Negative space carries as much weight as the frame. The composition remains centered, calm, and unusually legible, avoiding text and incidental detail. Repetition appears only as a faint technical grid, implying precision without turning the mark into a diagram.

Craftsmanship is expressed through exact optical balance, softened corners, clean antialiasing, and disciplined contrast. The finished icon should feel meticulously built by a specialist, not generated from a template. Every edge, offset, and highlight should survive reduction to a small home-screen glyph.

The final visual is a quiet architectural object: a window projected toward the viewer, a private path to another screen, and a premium mark whose confidence comes from restraint.
