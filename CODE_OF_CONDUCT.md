# Code of conduct

Oriel follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep criticism about the work, not the person. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated anywhere in this project's spaces.

To report a problem, email info@67enterprise.com. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept confidential.
